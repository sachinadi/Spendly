Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you register, along with the expense records, categories, amounts, and dates you log while using the Service. We may also collect basic technical data, such as browser type and login timestamps, to help keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide and improve the Service — for example, to display your expense history, generate spending summaries, and maintain your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party services for core functionality, such as hosting and font delivery. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. Spendly does not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided within the app.
